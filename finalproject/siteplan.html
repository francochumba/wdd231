Summer Equipment Rental Site Plan

Planning document for the individual website project.

Site Name

Summer Project Rentals

This name was selected because the website focuses on tools and equipment people can rent for summer home, garden, and outdoor projects. The name is simple, clear, and directly connected to the purpose of the website.

Optional domain: summerprojectrentals.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide information about summer tools and equipment available for rent. The site will help visitors understand what each tool is used for, when it may be helpful, basic safety tips, and why renting can be a practical option instead of buying expensive equipment for short-term projects.

The website will include equipment such as lawn mowers, pressure washers, hedge trimmers, ladders, drills, blowers, and other tools commonly used for home maintenance, yard care, cleaning, and outdoor repairs.

Scenarios

  • What equipment can I rent to clean my patio, driveway, or outdoor areas during summer?
  • Which tool should I choose for trimming bushes, cutting grass, or maintaining my yard?
  • How can renting equipment help me complete a home project without buying expensive tools?

Typography

The website will use Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif for all text. This font choice is simple, readable, and appropriate for an informational rental website.

  • Headings: Arial bold
  • Body text: Arial regular
  • Navigation and buttons: Arial bold
